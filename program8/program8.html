My Favourite Place

Munnar

Munnar is one of the most beautiful hill stations in kerala. It is famous for its green tea plantations,mountains,waterfalls and pleasant climate.

Munnar is a wonderful place to enjoy nature and spend time with family and friends. thebeautiful sceneryand peaceful atmosphere make it my favourite place.
